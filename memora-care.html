Features

🔔 Voice reminders

Reminders that sound even when the screen is off, and repeat until the task is marked as done.

🗣️ Voice assistant

Talks with the user and confirms when a task is completed.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family connection

Family members see task status in real time and can add tasks remotely.

📍 Emergency help

One tap shares the user's location with emergency contacts, plus quick calls to up to 5 contacts.

📅 Event calendar

Birthdays, appointments and special events with advance alerts.

📊 Reports

Two-week and monthly reports with trends and response times.

Memora Care is not a medical device. Task completion is reported by the user.
