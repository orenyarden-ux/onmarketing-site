My Eden Garden – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information the game My Eden Garden ("the game") collects and how it is used. The game is developed by Oren Yarden – On Marketing, Kfar Vradim, Israel ("we", "us").

Information we collect

  • Game progress. Your garden, currencies and settings are stored on your device. If you use cloud save, your game progress is stored in Google Firebase so you can restore it.
  • Location. If you turn on the optional "real weather" feature, the game uses your approximate location (city level only, never your precise location) to show the local weather in your garden. You can turn it off at any time in the game settings or in your device settings.
  • Advertising. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address) and information about ad interactions, in order to show and measure ads.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and basic technical data (device model, OS version) may be collected to fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number in the game.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob – Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase – Firebase privacy information

Your choices

  • Ad privacy choices: in the game settings, players in the EU/UK can review and change their consent for personalized ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings.
  • You can ask us to delete your cloud-saved data by writing to the email below.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
